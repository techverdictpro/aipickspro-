Al-Kholood host Al-Fayha in a Thursday evening fixture where the razor-thin margin in the odds reflects two teams locked in a battle for mid-table respectability, with just 0.17 separating the home and away prices in what bookmakers clearly view as a genuine coin-flip encounter.
