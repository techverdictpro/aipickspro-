Minnesota's Home Court Faces Stern Test as Jokic's Nuggets Roll Into Town

Nikola Jokic and the Denver Nuggets arrive at Target Center as heavy 1.40 favorites, a price that reflects both their superior regular season performance and their historical dominance in this Western Conference rivalry.

The Nuggets, meanwhile, continue to execute their Jokic-centric system with ruthless efficiency, making them one of the most difficult teams to game-plan against in the league.

The deciding factor comes down to Denver's half-court execution against Minnesota's switching defense. When the Timberwolves switch everything, Jokic hunts the smaller defender; when they don't switch, the Nuggets find open shooters. This tactical checkmate has defined the matchup for three seasons, and nothing suggests Minnesota has found an answer.

The 1.40 price on Denver reflects genuine probability rather than market overreaction.
