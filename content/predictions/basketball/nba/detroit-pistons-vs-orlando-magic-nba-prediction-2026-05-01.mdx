Detroit Pistons' Transformation From Laughingstock to Legitimate Contender Faces Orlando Test

The gap between these franchises, once a chasm, has narrowed considerably — though the oddsmakers suggest Detroit has pulled decisively ahead.

Without current season statistics available, historical context becomes essential. Head-to-head meetings between these Central Florida and Michigan franchises have produced competitive contests, with home court proving decisive in most recent encounters.

The deciding factor here is pace manipulation.
